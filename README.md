# 🩸 Rakt Setu – Smart Blood Donation Platform  

Rakt Setu is a **smart, secure, and inclusive blood-donor platform** built for real-time donor availability, trusted verification, and emergency-ready support. It leverages **AI-powered donor matching, health validation, and community engagement features** to ensure safe, reliable, and timely blood donations.  

---

## 🚀 Features  

- 🔎 **Smart Search** – Instantly find donors by area, distance, and blood group.  
- ✅ **Verified Donors** – Secure registration with health validation for safe donations.  
- 🚨 **Emergency Mode** – Broadcasts instant alerts to nearby eligible donors.  
- 🏆 **Motivation & Rewards** – Points, badges, free checkups, and recognition to engage donors.  
- ❤️ **Community Awareness** – Organizes blood donation camps, events, and awareness drives.  
- 📊 **Health Tracking Dashboard** – Monitors donor vitals & donation history for safe repeat donations.  
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Smart Matching** – Suggests best donor matches using health, location, and history data.  
- 🔐 **Secure & Inclusive Platform** – Ensures data privacy, accessibility, and multi-language support.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  

- **Frontend:** React.js, HTML5, CSS3  
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js  
- **Database:** MongoDB  
- **Cloud Platform:** AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda)  
- **AI/ML:** Python (TensorFlow / PyTorch)  
- **Mapping Service:** Google Maps API  

---

## 📌 Use Cases  

- 🏥 Hospitals handling emergency blood requirements.  
- 🌐 NGOs & Red Cross during disaster relief.  
- 🎓 Community donation drives at colleges, corporates, and public events.

## 💡 Benefits  

- **Healthcare:** Faster blood availability, verified and safe donors.  
- **Donors:** Health tracking, rewards, and motivation to donate regularly.  
- **Hospitals/Community:** Reliable donor network, reduced cost of blood drives.  
- **Social Impact:** Stronger community engagement, saving more lives.
